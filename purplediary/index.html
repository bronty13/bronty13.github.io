PurpleDiary

A private, encrypted journal that lives entirely on your Mac — no account, no cloud, and no network access, ever.

Download for macOS

Version 1.0.1680 · release notes

Install it

  1. Click Download above — you'll get a .dmg disk image.
  2. Open the DMG and drag PurpleDiary.app into the Applications folder shown next to it.
  3. Open it from Applications. On first launch it creates your encrypted diary and hands you a 24-word recovery key — write that down and keep it safe; it's the only way back in if your Mac's Keychain is ever lost.
Why is there no auto-update? By design. PurpleDiary makes zero network requests — no update checks, no telemetry, nothing — so your journal's privacy guarantee stays literally true. To update, come back to this page, download the newest DMG, and drag the app over the old one. Your entries are untouched (they live in your Library folder, not in the app).

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on an Apple Silicon Mac. The app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings — even offline.
